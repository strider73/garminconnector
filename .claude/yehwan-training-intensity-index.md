## Training Philosophy

Yehwan's data reveals a **periodized, balanced approach:**

1. **Recovery Built In:** 39% of days are rest/light (<600 cal)
2. **High Intensity When Needed:** 37% of days are hard/very hard (>1000 cal)
3. **Not Constant Grinding:** Varies intensity throughout the week
4. **Tournament Peaks:** Very hard days (>1500 cal) typically tournament/competition days

This approach prevents burnout and allows for proper recovery while maintaining high performance.

## Using This Data

**For AI Coaching:**
- Use active calories to assess daily training load
- Compare today's calories to typical ranges for intensity classification
- Multiple consecutive hard days (>1000 cal) = overreaching risk
- 3+ consecutive days >1500 cal = high injury risk

**For Training Planning:**
- Target weekly distribution matches historical pattern
- Don't exceed 2 consecutive very hard days
- Plan rest/light days after hard training blocks
- Monitor acute load trends over 7-day periods

**Red Flags:**
- >1500 cal for 3+ consecutive days
- Acute load >1300 (90th percentile - excessive)
- <300 cal for 7+ consecutive days (detraining risk)
